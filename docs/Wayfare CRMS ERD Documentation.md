# Wayfare CRMS: ERD Documentation

Car Rental Management System (backend: ASP.NET Core, EF Core, ASP.NET Identity)

## 1. Overview

This document describes the entity relationship diagram (ERD) of the Wayfare car rental system. The model has six entities: `ApplicationUser`, `Customer`, `Branch`, `Car`, `CarImage` and `Rental`. Customers register through ASP.NET Identity, browse cars that belong to branches and available at a selected time period and create rentals for a car at a branch. Cars carry several enum attributes for type, fuel, transmission and status.

The ERD draws each relationship as a rectangle between the two entities it connects. Cardinality is written `1` (one) or `m` (many) next to each entity. Participation is shown by the line type: a double line means full (total) participation, so every record of that entity must take part in the relationship, and a single line means partial participation, so a record may have no match. Attributes marked with a diamond (◆) hold an enum value, `PK` marks a primary key and `FK` a foreign key.

## 2. Entity summary

| Entity | Purpose | Primary key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ApplicationUser | Identity account used to log in (admins and customers) | Id (string) |
| Customer | Customer profile and driving license data | Id (int) |
| Branch | A physical rental branch where cars are kept | Id (int) |
| Car | A rentable vehicle | Id (int) |
| CarImage | A photo of a car (multivalued attribute of Car) | Id (int) |
| Rental | A booking of one car by one customer at one branch | Id (int) |

## 4. Relationships

| # | Relationship | Entities | Cardinality | Participation | Meaning | Foreign key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Login as | ApplicationUser, Customer | 1 : 1 | ApplicationUser full, Customer full | A user logs in as one customer profile | Customer.UserId |
| 2 | Has | Customer, Rental | 1 : m | Customer partial, Rental full | A customer has many rentals, and may have none yet | Rental.CustomerId |
| 3 | Has | Car, Rental | 1 : m | Car partial, Rental full | A car has many rentals, and may have none yet | Rental.CarId |
| 4 | Belong to | Branch, Car | 1 : m | Branch full, Car full | A branch holds many cars, and every car belongs to a branch | Car.BranchId |
| 5 | Has | Branch, Rental | 1 : m | Branch partial, Rental full | A branch handles many rentals, and may have none yet | Rental.BranchId |
| 6 | Has images | Car, CarImage | 1 : m | Car full, CarImage full | A car has many images, and every image belongs to a car | CarImage.CarId |

In every `1 : m` relationship the foreign key sits on the "many" side. In the `1 : 1` relationship it sits on Customer.

Rental has full participation in all three of its relationships, so a rental always has a customer, a car and a branch. Customer, Car and Branch have partial participation with Rental, so each of them can exist before it has any rentals.

## 6. Business rules shown by the model

- Each customer profile is tied to exactly one login account.
- A car always belongs to one branch, and a rental always references one customer, one car and one branch.
- A car can have several images, with one marked as the main image and the rest ordered by `DisplayOrder`.
- `JoinedAtDate` and `CreatedAtDate` are set once when the record is created and are not edited afterwards.
- A rental moves through the statuses in `RentalStatus`, starting at Requested.
- The amount owing on a rental can be derived from `TotalAmount`, `PaidAmount` and `RefundedAmount`.
